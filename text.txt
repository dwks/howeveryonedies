Advanced AI has the potential to be extremely dangerous, and by continuing to
develop it we run the risk of catastrophic outcomes up to and including human
extinction.

A common response to learning about catastrophic AI risk is to ask, how could
that actually happen? How could an AI inside a computer cause humans to die,
let alone all of humanity? This site answers these questions with example
scenarios.

Note that these are examples to illustrate potential dangers. We couldn't know
exactly how a superintelligence AI would kill everyone, the same way you
couldn't predict in advance how you might lose to a chess grandmaster. However,
you could be pretty sure that you would lose.
