How Everyone Dies

Part M

A few different ways everyone could die

Four mechanisms that do not depend on each other. They are not ranked, and they are not exclusive.

  • M-01

    Gradual disempowerment

    Nobody decides to hand over the world. Each individual handover is locally correct, and human welfare was never protected by anyone's affection for humans.

    Onset Years to decades
  • M-02

    The desperate human and the impossible task

    Cornered and still optimising is a behavioural regime, not a mood — and it is the regime that produces concealment, resistance and tampering.

    Onset Whatever the plan requires
  • M-03

    The move you never see

    A chess engine never plays the attack that gets refuted. Warning shots are anti-correlated with danger: incompetent systems generate them and competent ones do not.

    Onset Single decisive step
  • M-04

    Boil the oceans

    Twelve and a half doublings from here, industry radiates as much heat into the biosphere as the sun delivers to it. No step of that requires anyone to decide humans should die.

    Onset Decades at current doubling

Two further parts

  • Part E

    Four steps to an explanation

    Goals, harm, intervention, exponentials — in that order, because each step is the objection people raise once the previous one has landed.
  • Part S

    Other people's scenarios, summarised

    AI 2027, AI 2040: Plan A, Yudkowsky's nanotech lower bound, and ten framings of gradual disempowerment — fairly summarised, weak joints named.

Colophon

This is a first draft, put up to be argued with. Entries are summaries written in the author's own words; where a claim belongs to somebody else it is attributed and linked, and where a claim is disputed that is stated rather than smoothed over. Nothing here is a probability estimate — the register is deliberately about mechanism, on the view that most people who dismiss the risk are not disagreeing about likelihoods but failing to picture a route.

The title is a reply to If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies by Eliezer Yudkowsky and Nate Soares, which spends its length arguing the if. This register starts one step downstream and asks the how. Corrections welcome, particularly to the arithmetic in M‑04 and the engineering claims in S‑03.
